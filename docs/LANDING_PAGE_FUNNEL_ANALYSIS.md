# Landing Page Funnel Analysis — Blinkify vs TryHolo.ai

## Goal
Get visitors to want to register for a free trial in **under 2 minutes**.

---

| 1 | Hero | Promise + CTA ✓ |
| 2 | Companies (logos) | Trust — **too early** (visitor doesn’t know what they’re trusting yet) |
| 3 | Macbook (before/after) | Product proof ✓ |
| 4 | Visual Demo (marquee) | More proof ✓ |
| 5 | How It Works | Process ✓ |
| 6 | Who It’s For | Audience fit ✓ |
| 7 | Value (Built for Your Brand) | Benefits ✓ |
| 8 | AI Creatives Carousel | Showcase (long, late) |

**Problems:** Trust (logos) before proof; stats and strongest “wow” (product demo) buried; long path before pricing; no FAQ to handle objections.

## Recommended Blinkify Flow (Optimized for <2 min)

| Order | Section | Why this position |
|-------|---------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Hero** | One promise, one CTA. Add trust line under button: “Free trial · No credit card.” |
| 2 | **Macbook (product proof)** | Show the transformation **immediately** so “10x faster” is visual, not just copy. |
| 3 | **Stats Bento** | 10x, $2,400, 85%, 500+ right after “wow” — builds desire and credibility fast. |
| 4 | **Companies** | Logos after proof + stats so “companies leveraging Gen AI” has context. |
| 5 | **Visual Demo** | Second proof moment; reinforces “see the transformation.” |
| 6 | **How It Works** | 3 steps after interest is built. |
| 7 | **Who It’s For** | Self-identification (Small Biz, eCommerce, Agencies). |
| 8 | **Value (Built for Your Brand)** | Benefits and differentiation. |
| 9 | **AI Creatives Carousel** | “More examples” — keeps interest, not decision-critical. |
| 10 | **Testimonials** | Social proof right before price. |
| 11 | **Pricing** | Clear plans + “Start Free Trial” on each. |
| 12 | **Final CTA** | Last push. |

**Removed / merged:** Nothing removed. One reorder: Stats and product proof (Macbook) moved up; Companies after proof.

**Add:** (1) Trust line under hero CTA. (2) FAQ block (3–5 questions: free trial, what formats, data privacy, cancel anytime).

## Why This Flow Can Outperform Holo (While Learning From Them)

1. **Proof before trust**  
   Blinkify shows the product (before/after, then stats) before “companies leveraging Gen AI.” Holo leads with credentials. For a “10x faster” creative tool, **seeing the result** in under 30 seconds creates desire; logos then reinforce instead of carrying the whole load.

2. **Stats right after the “wow”**  
   Moving 10x, $2,400 saved, 85% time reduction, 500+ variations to immediately after the Macbook section turns the first scroll into: see it → believe the numbers → then see who else uses it. Holo’s stats sit later; earlier stats support a faster “this is real” moment.

3. **Free trial as primary CTA**  
   “Start Free Trial” lowers friction vs “Buy now.” For <2 min to “want to register,” trial is a stronger first step; Holo’s strength is clarity (one action), not friction level.

4. **Pricing on the page**  
   Showing 3 plans + “Start Free Trial” on each removes a click and answers “how much?” without leaving the page. Holo sends to /pricing; inline pricing can shorten the path to signup.

5. **Same strengths as Holo**  
   Short “how it works,” clear “who it’s for,” strong testimonials before CTA, and FAQ for objections — we keep these, with order tuned so proof and numbers come first.

6. **Fewer sections before “want to try”**  
   By placing product proof and stats in the first 3 sections, the “I get it and I want it” moment happens in the first minute; the rest of the page supports and closes. Holo is longer before the first strong proof moment.

**Summary:** Holo converts well with trust-first and a single “Buy now” path. Blinkify’s recommended flow is **proof-first + numbers + free trial + pricing on page**, which is better aligned with a “see it, believe it, try it in under 2 minutes” goal. Implementing the section reorder (and adding hero trust line + FAQ) applies this structure.
