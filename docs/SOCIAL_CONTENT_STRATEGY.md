## 2. Content Ideas by Format & Audience

### LinkedIn (B2B, founders, ecom/marketing leads)
- **Pain + stat posts:** “Your ad creatives aren’t slow—your process is.” + stat (e.g. 85% time reduction) + one CTA.
- **Before/after carousels:** One product, 3–5 slides (raw → Blinkify output), caption = one benefit + link.
- **Agency/solopreneur story:** “We went from 2 days per client to 2 hours. Here’s the stack.”
- **Myth vs reality:** “You need a designer to get scroll-stopping product ads. You don’t.”
- **Single-tip threads:** “5 ways we cut creative production time (without hiring).”

### X / Twitter (builders, indie hackers, ecom)
- **Hot take + proof:** “Product photos don’t need a photoshoot. They need 15 seconds and AI.” + before/after image.
- **Number hooks:** “$2,400/month saved on creatives. Here’s how.”
- **Relatable pain:** “Still waiting on your designer for that product shot? There’s another way.”
- **Tool stack tweets:** “Our ad creative stack: [camera] → Blinkify → Meta/Google. No Photoshop.”

### Instagram (ecom, DTC, small biz)
- **Before/after Reels:** Raw product → Blinkify result in 15 sec; text overlay: “No shoot. No designer.”
- **UGC-style hooks:** “I was paying $X per product shot. Not anymore.”
- **Carousel:** Slide 1 = hook (“Stop paying for product photoshoots”), 2–5 = before/after, last = CTA.
- **Stories:** Poll “Product photo: shoot or AI?” → swipe up to try.

### TikTok (ecom, young SMB)
- **Speed demo:** “Product photo to ad in 15 seconds” with screen record + voiceover.
- **“I tried X so you don’t have to”:** “I tried 5 AI image tools for my store. This one actually matched my brand.”
- **Pattern interrupt:** Handwritten text: “My designer quit. So I did this.” → show Blinkify.

**Week 3 – Myth**
- Hook: “Everyone says you need a studio for product shots. Here’s why they’re wrong.”  
  Body: Before/after examples + “no designer needed.” CTA: “Try it free” link.

**Week 4 – Story**
- Hook: “We went from 2 days per client to 2 hours. Here’s the stack.”  
  Body: Agency/solopreneur angle + Blinkify in the stack. CTA: “Comment STACK and I’ll send it.”

**Rotate:** One pain, one stat, one myth-bust, one story. Test which CTA (comment vs link) drives more signups for your audience.
